On the ₹2 lakh itself, you've got two paths.

**Prepay the home loan.** It's costing you 9% on ₹40 lakh outstanding, over 18 years left. The tradeoff: once it's in the house, it's not easily pulled back out.

**Invest it instead.** You already run a SIP into an equity fund, so you have the mechanism in hand — money placed in the market aiming for a return above what the loan costs you. Equity returns over long periods have typically run somewhere in the 10–12% range, though nothing about that is guaranteed year to year, and the money stays liquid if you need it.
