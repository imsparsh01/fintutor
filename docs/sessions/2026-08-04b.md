**Headline findings:** Segment population (SAM) ≈ 30–55M (central ~38–42M), digital access is not a
constraint. Real competitive whitespace exists (no India player combines real-numbers personalization with
zero product recommendation) — but it's open specifically because the no-advice posture forecloses the
commission/distribution revenue model every funded competitor relies on (Fi Money's Feb-2026 consumer
shutdown, despite a bank partnership, is a concrete cautionary data point). The naive population-based SOM
(200K–1.2M users in 12–24mo) is not realistic for this team: a solo founder + AI-only team, organic
acquisition only, realistically reaches ~15K–60K registered users in 24 months — the bottleneck is founder
bandwidth on non-delegable trust functions (DPDP grievance officer, breach response, app-store account,
escalated support), not audience size or CAC. At that grounded scale, consumer subscription revenue is
likely immaterial (~$12.6K lifetime pool across a 24-month cohort, given India's ~1.4% conversion / ~$14
LTV benchmarks) — the B2B2C employer-benefit channel looks like the more plausible near-term lever.
