# D-054 — BRIEF-009 resolved: no sub-segment picked to start with — all three cuts treated as one population, fully in MVP scope

- **Tier:** owner-decided directly in conversation. No Tier-3 trigger fired (no new product type/screen/
  flow/capability beyond `PROJECT_SPEC.md` §4, no compliance/legal touch, no low-reversibility data
  touched, no standing principle contradicted) — logged as a full entry anyway because it directly shapes
  imminent onboarding/capture design work, same reasoning D-052 used.
- **Decision:** Owner judged that BRIEF-009's two independent cuts of the D-053 founding segment — Product
  lens (Fresh starters / Reactive dabblers / Habit-formers, by financial-habit maturity) and Business lens
  (Campus-to-first-job / Settled early-career / Early-stage startup-gig, by career-stage/timing) — describe
  the **same three populations from two angles**, not six distinct ones. On that basis: **no sub-segment is
  picked to start with.** All three are explicitly IN the MVP design and Phase-3 testing population; none
  deprioritized. This is the full internal breadth of D-053's founding segment ("early-career, single-income,
  low-complexity"), not a narrowing of it.
- **What this does NOT change:** D-053's founding-segment boundary itself is untouched — BRIEF-008's other
  parked candidates (fragmented multi-holding, gig/variable income, debt-heavy/reactive, household
  coordinators, dual-income households, first-jobbers-as-a-distinct-macro-segment, NRIs) remain parked for
  post-MVP, exactly as D-053 left them. This decision only concerns how the founding segment's internal
  diversity is treated, not the outer boundary.
- **Practical effect on design work:** the three sub-segment profiles are retained as internal design
  texture, not an audience filter — e.g. onboarding needs to work for someone with zero financial habits
  (fresh starter) *and* someone already budgeting in a spreadsheet (habit-former), without assuming either
  is the "real" target and the other an edge case.
- **Caveat, flagged not silently dropped:** BRIEF-009 explicitly called the Product/Business correlation "not
  verified, just the obvious correlation." This decision proceeds on the owner's judgment call that it holds
  well enough to act on. If real design work or Phase-3 testing later surfaces a population that breaks the
  assumed 1:1 mapping (e.g. a genuine "habit-former" who is also "campus-to-first-job," contradicting the
  pairing), that is new information warranting a revisit — not evidence this decision was wrong when made.
- **Reversibility:** High — a framing/design-input decision; nothing in `app/` or `backend/` is built against
  segment specifics yet.
