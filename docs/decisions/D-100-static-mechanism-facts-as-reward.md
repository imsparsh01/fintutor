# D-100 — Static mechanism facts are the variable reward

- **Tier:** 3, owner-approved directly in conversation
- **Decision:** When the existing app-open reward fires, the app shows one curated, mechanism-only fact selected from a bundled catalog. Selection is independent of user holdings, balances, goals, progress, and outcomes. The reward remains on the engagement plane and is dismissible.
- **Guardrails:** No XP, score, badge, level, financial performance claim, personalized number, or fact selected from baseline data. Facts use the tutor voice and explain mechanisms without recommending action.
- **Why:** Makes the variable reward educational while preserving P7/D-061’s separation between cosmetic engagement and real financial data.
- **Reversibility:** Client-only content and presentation; no new backend field or persistence.
- **Date:** 10-Aug-2026
