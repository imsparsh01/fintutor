# D-163 — Portfolio Health has no composite score

- **Tier:** 3, owner-decided financial-score meaning and product principle.
- **Supersedes:** D-105 Decision 4, D-106 Decision 2 overall-score rule, and D-111's overall-score summary.
- **Date:** 29-Aug-2026

## Decision

Portfolio Health shows its individual recorded-data mechanisms and measured/unknown states but no overall
0–100 number at any completeness level. Adding information can reveal or recalculate a measure, but cannot
make a headline grade rise or fall.

Portfolio/Home summaries name which measures are available and route to them; they do not average unlike
mechanisms or imply that an unmeasured area is zero.

## Why

A composite makes Investment rate, Insurance, Emergency buffer and Tax utilisation appear comparable and
equally important. A partial average changes meaning with its denominator, while a full average still reads
as a verdict. Separate measures are more explainable and better fit teach-never-advise.

## Boundaries

- Individual Investment rate, Emergency buffer and Tax utilisation calculations remain unchanged.
- D-162 replaces the former insurance-points formula with factual components.
- No production code changes until a separately bounded post-validation build.

## Reversibility

High in the controlled prototype. Production replacement is separately reviewed because committed screens
currently depend on the former score.
